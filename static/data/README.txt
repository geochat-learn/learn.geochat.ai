california-10-counties-healthcare-access-2026.csv
Published by GeoChat AI. Page: https://learn.geochat.ai/california-county-healthcare-access-2026/

Columns
- adults_65_plus, pct_65_plus: ACS 2020-2024 5-year estimates, table B01001.
- pct_uninsured_all_ages, pct_uninsured_65_plus: ACS 2020-2024 5-year, table B27010 (civilian noninstitutionalized population).
- primary_care_physicians_per_10k: individual NPIs whose primary taxonomy is family medicine, general internal medicine or pediatrics, NPPES October 2026 monthly file, per 10,000 residents (ACS 2020-2024 population). Counts are registrations, not full-time clinicians.
- primary_care_nps_per_10k: individual NPIs whose primary taxonomy is a primary care nurse practitioner code, same file and denominator.
- primary_care_hpsa_coverage, mental_health_hpsa_coverage: HRSA Health Professional Shortage Area data downloaded October 28, 2026 and rechecked against HRSA's November 2, 2026 files. "Entire county" = every land census tract lies inside at least one HPSA of that discipline (one countywide designation or several local ones); "Part of county" = some tracts do not; "Facilities only" = only facility HPSAs. Designations proposed for withdrawal are counted.
- primary_care_hpsa_highest_score: highest score among the county's geographic and population primary care HPSAs, including those proposed for withdrawal; facility HPSAs excluded. Scale 0-25, higher = greater need.
- notes: where a value rests on a designation proposed for withdrawal, or an estimate is unreliable.
