ArcGIS is a professional GIS platform for almost any spatial analysis, while GeoChat AI is a free chat tool for healthcare supply-demand questions.

ArcGIS gives you far more analytical depth and control. GeoChat AI gives non-GIS staff a plain-language route to a narrower set of access questions.

## Side-by-side comparison

|---|---|---|
| What it is | Esri's GIS platform: ArcGIS Online on the web, [ArcGIS Pro](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-pro/overview) on the desktop, and [Business Analyst](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-business-analyst/overview) for market and site analysis | AI-powered mapping platform for community health assessments, needs assessments and healthcare supply-demand studies ([atlas.geochat.ai](https://atlas.geochat.ai)) |

## What ArcGIS includes

ArcGIS Online is Esri's web GIS, delivered as software as a service ([overview](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-online/overview)). ArcGIS Pro is the desktop application, and it [runs on Windows only](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-pro/overview).

### Licensing as of November 2026

Esri sells six [annual user types](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-online/buy). Creator, Professional and Professional Plus each include 500 ArcGIS Online credits; Mobile Worker and Contributor include 250; Viewer includes none. Professional adds ArcGIS Pro Standard, and Professional Plus adds ArcGIS Pro Advanced and extensions.

- **Free trial:** [21 days](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/trial), with 2 Creator and 3 Viewer user types and 400 credits.
- **Free public account:** allowed for [noncommercial use](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/arcgis-online/reference/faq.htm), with 2 GB of storage. It cannot run Map Viewer analysis, publish hosted layers or use premium content.
- **Personal Use license:** [limited to noncommercial use](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-for-personal-use/buy) by an individual, and excludes work for government or nonprofit entities.
- **Bundles:** a [Mapping and Analytics subscription](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/mapping-analytics-cloud-based-subscription/buy) for US commercial customers comes in Individual and "For Up to 20 Users" versions.
- **Nonprofit program:** [special pricing](https://www.esri.com/en-us/industries/nonprofit/buy) for approved nonprofit organizations doing mission-driven, noncommercial work.

### Health content

Esri's [health overview](https://www.esri.com/en-us/industries/health/overview) lists focus areas including health equity and access to health care and services. Living Atlas includes [HPSA layers](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/arcgis-living-atlas/health/hpsas) for primary care, dental and mental health, checked monthly against HRSA. Esri's 2022 blog also describes a [CDC PLACES layer](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/arcgis-living-atlas/health/cdc-places) for counties, places, tracts and ZCTAs.

Living Atlas content comes in [three access levels](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-enterprise/latest/administer/configure-living-atlas-content.html). Public content needs no sign-in, subscriber content needs an organizational subscription, and premium content consumes credits.

### Business Analyst for health market and site work

The Business Analyst Web App Standard is [included in Creator, Professional and Professional Plus](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-business-analyst/buy). Web App Advanced, a separate purchase, adds suitability, void and threshold area analysis.

In [June 2026](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/esri-demographics/analytics/esri-demographics-whats-new-june-2026), Esri Demographics added seven healthcare business categories from Data Axle. They include Physicians and Doctors, Dentists, and Home Health Care Services.

## When to choose ArcGIS

ArcGIS is the stronger choice when you need analytical depth, custom methods, or a full product family with its own data library and training.

- **You have GIS staff or need custom analysis.** Esri says ArcGIS Pro lets you "perform any type of spatial analysis," and ArcGIS Network Analyst is included with the [Advanced license](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-pro/overview).
- **You are siting a clinic or defining a service area.** Business Analyst offers [drive time, trade area rings and market potential](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-business-analyst/overview) (see [drive-time access](/glossary/drive-time-access)).
- **You need provider-type business counts.** Esri's [healthcare business categories](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/esri-demographics/analytics/esri-demographics-whats-new-june-2026) cover physicians, dentists and home health services.
- **You need fine geography.** Esri Demographics added [block-group ACS variables](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/esri-demographics/analytics/esri-demographics-whats-new-june-2026) that were previously available only down to tracts.
- **You publish for the public.** ArcGIS Online builds [dashboards, apps and stories](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-online/overview) you can share.
- **You want natural-language help inside GIS.** The [Business Analyst assistant](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/business-analyst/web/use-business-analyst-assistant.htm) responds to prompts, creates maps and suggests reports. Your administrator must enable AI first.

## When to choose GeoChat AI

GeoChat AI fits when you need answers about healthcare supply and demand, and your team does not run GIS software.

- **You want to ask, not build.** You type a question, then follow up, drill into ZIP codes or compare regions in the same chat.
- **Your focus is access gaps.** Its interactive maps show supply-demand gaps, provider deserts and population health patterns.
- **Your program matches its use cases.** The platform offers CHNA, personal care and home health, PACE, rural health, community health worker and workforce analysis use cases.
- **You need a no-cost start.** The [free tier](https://atlas.geochat.ai) needs no credit card or license purchase.
- **You want expert help.** [Paid services](https://geochat.ai) cover expert review of AI findings, primary research and community interviews, custom needs assessment reports, methodology design and stakeholder presentation support.
- **You need a map that doesn't exist yet.** Nonprofit organizations can request a new map, and those requests shape what gets added.

### GeoChat AI limits

- It is a new platform; the current version is GeoChat Atlas (version 2).
- Provider counts are NPPES registrations, not full-time clinicians, so they overstate available capacity.
- It is not a general-purpose GIS. If you need custom network, drive-time or suitability analysis, use ArcGIS.

## Using both

The tools fit different stages of an access study.

1. Use GeoChat AI to scope the question and find which ZIP codes or regions show supply-demand gaps.
2. Hand the shortlist to your GIS analyst for detailed work in ArcGIS, such as drive-time areas or clinic siting.
3. Add Living Atlas HPSA layers to check whether flagged areas are already designated shortage areas (see [HPSA](/glossary/hpsa)).
4. Confirm both sides use the same ACS release and geography (see [ACS 5-year estimates](/glossary/acs-5-year-estimates) and [ZCTA vs ZIP code](/glossary/zcta-vs-zip-code)).

## How to choose

- Pick **ArcGIS** if you have GIS staff, need custom methods, or must publish dashboards and apps.
- Pick **ArcGIS Business Analyst** if your question is site selection, trade areas or market potential for a health service.
- Pick **GeoChat AI** if your team has no GIS staff and needs to explore healthcare access gaps by asking questions.
- Check whether your organization already holds ArcGIS user types before buying new licenses.
- Nonprofits should apply to Esri's [nonprofit program](https://www.esri.com/en-us/industries/nonprofit/buy) before buying.
- Avoid the Personal Use license for agency work, since it [excludes government and nonprofit use](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-for-personal-use/buy).
- To discuss GeoChat AI's expert services, [book a call](https://calendly.com/v-wong-geochat/30min).
