PolicyMap is a broad subscription platform for health, housing and economic data, while GeoChat AI is a free, chat-based tool for healthcare supply-demand gaps.

PolicyMap offers far more data and analysis tools. GeoChat AI offers a narrower, no-cost way to ask access questions in plain language.

## What PolicyMap is

PolicyMap describes itself as "a mapping and analytics platform" that collects, cleans and maintains data for its customers ([PolicyMap](https://www.policymap.com/)). Its subscription options include the mapping platform, data licensing by flat file, API or Snowflake, embedded maps, partner portals and consulting ([pricing](https://www.policymap.com/pricing)).

For community health, PolicyMap says users can build CHNAs, strategic plans and primary care needs assessments ([community health page](https://www.policymap.com/industries/community-health)). That page names Maricopa County and Northwell Health among its health customers.

The provider counts group NUCC taxonomy codes into specialties (see [NPI taxonomy codes](/glossary/npi-taxonomy-codes)). PolicyMap leaves out point HPSAs, saying they "may not be reflective of the larger population or geographic area" ([HRSA data](https://policymap.com/data/sources/hrsa)).

## When to choose PolicyMap

PolicyMap is the stronger choice when you need breadth, specialty-level provider counts or analysis tools beyond a single health question.

- **You need provider counts grouped by specialty.** Its [NPPES dataset](https://policymap.com/data/sources/cms-national-plan-and-provider-enumeration-system-nppes-data) groups taxonomy codes into specialties at the state and ZIP code level.
- **You need to combine many factors.** Multi-layer maps overlay [up to five data layers](https://www.policymap.com/products/mapping-analytics) and show where conditions align.
- **You need to map your own addresses.** The [geocoder](https://www.policymap.com/products/mapping-analytics) takes uploads and lets you review match rates and fix unmatched records.
- **Your work goes beyond health.** Indicators span demographics, housing, education, income, employment and environment ([data](https://www.policymap.com/data)).
- **You need data delivery or integrations.** PolicyMap licenses data by [flat file, API or Snowflake](https://www.policymap.com/pricing) and offers a Salesforce integration.
- **You want vendor support.** Subscribers get [weekly training, email and phone support](https://www.policymap.com/pricing), plus the Pam helpbot.

## When to choose GeoChat AI

GeoChat AI fits when your question is specifically about healthcare supply and demand, and you want to start without a purchase.

- **You need a no-cost start.** The [free tier](https://atlas.geochat.ai) needs no credit card. PolicyMap's [pricing page](https://www.policymap.com/pricing) lists quoted subscriptions only.
- **You want to ask, not build.** You type a question, then follow up, drill into ZIP codes or compare regions in the same chat.
- **Your focus is access gaps.** Its interactive maps show supply-demand gaps, provider deserts and population health patterns.
- **Your program matches its use cases.** The platform offers CHNA, personal care and home health, PACE, rural health, community health worker and workforce analysis use cases.
- **You want expert help.** [Paid services](https://geochat.ai) cover expert review of AI findings, primary research and community interviews, custom needs assessment reports, methodology design and stakeholder presentation support.
- **You need a map that doesn't exist yet.** Nonprofit organizations can request a new map, and those requests shape what gets added.

### GeoChat AI limits

- It is a new platform; the current version is GeoChat Atlas (version 2).
- Provider counts are NPPES registrations, not full-time clinicians, so they overstate available capacity.
- Its data centers on healthcare supply and demand, built from public sources such as ACS and NPPES. PolicyMap's catalog is far broader.

## Using both

The tools can work in sequence when budget or time is tight.

1. Use GeoChat AI's free tier to scope the question by drilling into ZIP codes and comparing regions.
2. Ask your university or public library whether it already subscribes to PolicyMap, since PolicyMap serves [academic and public library](https://www.policymap.com/) users.
3. In PolicyMap, pull provider counts by ZIP code and tract-level HPSA layers for the areas you flagged.
4. Check that both tools use the same ACS release before comparing population figures (see [ACS 5-year estimates](/glossary/acs-5-year-estimates)).
5. Match geographies carefully, since ZIP codes and ZCTAs differ ([ZCTA vs ZIP code](/glossary/zcta-vs-zip-code)).

## How to choose

- Pick **PolicyMap** if you need specialty-level provider counts, custom regions or data across housing and the economy.
- Pick **PolicyMap** if your organization needs data licensing, an API or a Salesforce integration.
- Pick **GeoChat AI** if you want to explore healthcare access gaps by asking questions, at no cost.
- Pick **GeoChat AI** if you also want paid expert help with interviews, methodology or a custom needs assessment report.
