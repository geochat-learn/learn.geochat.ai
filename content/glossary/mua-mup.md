A Medically Underserved Area (MUA) or Population (MUP) is a HRSA designation for an area or group lacking access to primary care ([HRSA](https://bhw.hrsa.gov/workforce-shortage-areas/shortage-designation)). HRSA scores both with the Index of Medical Underservice (IMU).

## MUA, MUP and Exceptional MUP

- **MUA:** a primary care shortage across a geographic area ([HRSA](https://bhw.hrsa.gov/workforce-shortage-areas/shortage-designation)). Examples are a whole county, a group of neighboring counties, a group of urban census tracts, or a group of county or civil divisions.
- **MUP:** a primary care shortage for a specific population subset within a geographic area.
- **Exceptional MUP:** a population that does not qualify as an MUP, but where unusual circumstances prevent access to primary care services.

## How the Index of Medical Underservice works

The IMU ranges from 0 to 100 ([HRSA](https://bhw.hrsa.gov/workforce-shortage-areas/shortage-designation/scoring)). An area or population with an IMU of 62.0 or below qualifies for designation, so lower scores mean greater underservice.

| IMU component | Maximum points |
|---|---|
| Providers per 1,000 population | 28.7 |
| Percent of population at 100% of the federal poverty level | 25.1 |
| Percent of population age 65 and over | 20.2 |
| Infant mortality rate | 26 |

The four maximums sum to 100 ([HRSA](https://bhw.hrsa.gov/workforce-shortage-areas/shortage-designation/scoring)).

## How MUA/P differs from a HPSA

| | HPSA | MUA/P |
|---|---|---|
| Disciplines | Primary care, dental, mental health | Primary care |
| Types | Geographic, population, facility (including auto-HPSAs) | Area, population, Exceptional MUP |
| Core test | Population-to-provider ratio thresholds in [42 CFR Part 5](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-5/appendix-Appendix%20A%20to%20Part%205) | IMU of 62.0 or below |
| Score | 0–25 (dental 0–26), calculated after designation | IMU, 0–100 |
| Programs HRSA lists | NHSC, Nurse Corps, IHS loan repayment, CMS HPSA bonus payments, Rural Health Clinic program, J-1 visa waiver (geographic primary care HPSAs) | Health Center Program, J-1 visa waiver; Rural Health Clinic program for MUAs |
| Lookup tool | [HPSA Find](https://data.hrsa.gov/tools/shortage-area/hpsa-find) | [MUA Find](https://data.hrsa.gov/tools/shortage-area/mua-find) |

Sources for scores and programs: [HRSA scoring](https://bhw.hrsa.gov/workforce-shortage-areas/shortage-designation/scoring) and [HRSA shortage designation](https://bhw.hrsa.gov/workforce-shortage-areas/shortage-designation). HRSA says MUA/P designations help establish community health centers and health maintenance organizations.

## Data source

[MUA Find](https://data.hrsa.gov/tools/shortage-area/mua-find) on data.hrsa.gov looks up designations by location or identifier. It filters by status, type and rural status. HRSA's scoring page points users to data.hrsa.gov for MUA/P scores ([HRSA](https://bhw.hrsa.gov/workforce-shortage-areas/shortage-designation/scoring)).

## Common pitfalls

- **Reading the IMU backward.** A low IMU signals need. HPSA scores work the other way: NHSC reviews applications in descending HPSA score order ([NHSC](https://nhsc.hrsa.gov/loan-repayment/funding-priorities)).
- **Applying it to dental or mental health.** MUA/P measures primary care access only.
- **Treating an MUP as area-wide.** An MUP covers a population subset, not every resident.
- **Using the designation as current data.** A designation reflects the data used when it was made. Recompute supply, poverty, age and infant mortality before describing today's conditions.
- **Assuming HPSA and MUA/P overlap.** They come from different tests, so check both layers.

## Using MUA/P in needs-assessment work

1. Pull MUA/P and HPSA records for the same service area and map them together.
2. Use the four IMU components as an indicator checklist. Draw poverty and age from [ACS 5-year estimates](/glossary/acs-5-year-estimates) and supply from [NPI data](/glossary/npi-taxonomy-codes).
3. In aging-services work, note that the share of residents age 65 and over is one of the four IMU inputs.
4. If a partner plans a new health center site, check MUA/P status early, since the Health Center Program uses these designations ([HRSA](https://bhw.hrsa.gov/workforce-shortage-areas/shortage-designation)).
